Our Story

The bakery-cafe "Cimet" started as a small idea in 2022, with a desire to create a warm and peaceful place where the aroma of freshly baked pastries and ground coffee fills the space. Every day, we prepare homemade desserts, artisan coffee, and healthy meals using the highest quality ingredients.
